Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-10
  Features:
    - Merged in bits from SergiX44's Atomic Artillery Reborn that I want to keep.
    - Added the Atomic Artillery research from SergiX44's Atomic Artillery Reborn. It requires atomic bomb, artillery and space science pack research.
  Balancing:
    - The explosion now copies the vanilla atomic rocket's effect when the game loads, from Atomic Artillery Reborn, so it follows vanilla changes.
    - The extra fire blast now does 2000 damage, up from 1500.
    - Atomic artillery shells weigh 300 kg, so 3 fit in a rocket. Yes, a vanilla atomic bomb is too heavy for a rocket. No, that isn't sensible, thus I ignore that. The one bad call by Factorio devs, ever ;-)
  Graphics:
    - 64x64 item icon from Atomic Artillery Reborn.
  Sounds:
    - Added the vanilla heavy artillery shell pickup, drop and move sounds.
  Locale:
    - Added Russian text for the research.
